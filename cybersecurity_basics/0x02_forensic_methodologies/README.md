# Forensic Ethics & Methodologies

Digital forensics is the intersection of law and computer science. This project explores the rigorous methodologies required to investigate digital crimes, the critical importance of ethical conduct, and the standardized processes that ensure digital evidence is remains admissible in a court of law.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this project, you should be able to explain the following concepts clearly without the help of a search engine:

### Ethics & Integrity

* **Defining Digital Forensics:** What it is and why it is vital to modern justice.
* **The Ethical Compass:** Why ethics are non-negotiable and the common ethical dilemmas faced by investigators.
* **Integrity & Objectivity:** How to maintain an unbiased perspective and ensure findings are based strictly on data.

### Methodologies & Standards

* **ACPO Principles:** The four core principles for handling digital evidence.
* **Chain of Custody:** How to document the chronological history of evidence to prevent tampering.
* **Admissibility:** Understanding the legal requirements for evidence to be used in court.
* **Standard Methodologies:** Following established frameworks (like NIST or DFRWS) to ensure repeatable results.

### The Forensic Process

* **Stages of Investigation:** From initial preservation and collection to analysis and reporting.
* **Evidence Handling:** Best practices for preserving the original state of digital media (e.g., using write-blockers).
* **Documentation:** How to write a forensic report that is technically accurate and understandable to non-experts.

## 🛠️ The Digital Forensic Process

| Phase | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Identification** | Recognizing the scene and identifying potential evidence sources. |
| **Preservation** | Protecting the evidence from alteration or destruction (Physical & Logical). |
| **Collection** | Gathering the evidence using forensic imaging techniques. |
| **Analysis** | Using specialized tools to extract and interpret data from the images. |
| **Reporting** | Documenting findings in a clear, objective, and legally sound report. |
